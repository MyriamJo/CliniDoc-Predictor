Procedure: 
Barium Swallow

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with chronic cough and suspected aspiration.

Operative Details:

Barium swallow imaging was performed with the patient in a seated position. The patient ingested a barium sulfate suspension, and fluoroscopic images were obtained during swallowing maneuvers. Imaging revealed evidence of aspiration with barium entering the trachea during the swallowing process, indicating impaired swallowing coordination.

Impression:

Barium swallow findings consistent with aspiration during swallowing, raising concern for dysphagia or neurological dysfunction.

Post-procedure Care:

Following the procedure, the patient was referred to a speech-language pathologist for further evaluation of swallowing function and rehabilitation. Dietary modifications, such as thickening liquids and modifying food textures, were recommended to reduce the risk of aspiration. Referral to a neurologist for assessment of underlying neurological conditions was also considered.

Follow-up:

A multidisciplinary approach involving speech therapy and neurology was planned to address swallowing dysfunction and minimize aspiration risk. Long-term management would focus on rehabilitation strategies and prevention of aspiration-related complications.
