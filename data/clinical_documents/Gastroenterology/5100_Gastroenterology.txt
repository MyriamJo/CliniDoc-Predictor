Procedure:
Barium Swallow

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with odynophagia and suspected esophageal inflammation or injury.

Operative Details:

Barium swallow imaging was performed with the patient in a supine position. The patient ingested a barium sulfate suspension, and fluoroscopic images were obtained during swallowing. Imaging revealed evidence of irregular mucosal lining and focal areas of narrowing in the mid-esophagus, suggestive of esophagitis or esophageal injury.

Impression:

Barium swallow findings consistent with esophagitis or esophageal injury, warranting further evaluation for underlying etiology.

Post-procedure Care:

Following the procedure, the patient was instructed to avoid irritants such as spicy foods and NSAIDs to prevent exacerbation of symptoms. Proton pump inhibitors and mucosal protectants were prescribed to reduce inflammation and promote healing. Dietary modifications, including a soft diet, were recommended to minimize discomfort during swallowing.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled with a gastroenterologist for endoscopic evaluation and biopsy to determine the underlying cause of esophageal inflammation or injury. Long-term management would focus on treating the underlying condition and preventing recurrence of esophagitis.
