Procedure: Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with acute cholecystitis, manifesting as severe right upper quadrant abdominal pain, fever, and leukocytosis.

Operative Details:

An urgent open cholecystectomy was performed under general anesthesia. The surgeon carefully dissected the gallbladder, which was found to be acutely inflamed. The cystic duct and artery were ligated, and the gallbladder was removed.

Impression:

Successful open cholecystectomy performed for acute cholecystitis with no intraoperative complications.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was closely monitored in the recovery area for any signs of postoperative complications such as bleeding, infection, or bile leakage. Pain management and intravenous fluids were administered as needed to ensure comfort and hydration.

Instructions were provided for postoperative care, including gradual advancement of diet and avoidance of heavy lifting or strenuous activity during the initial recovery period.

Follow-up:

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment with the surgical team to assess postoperative progress, review pathology results, and address any concerns or questions regarding the procedure. Long-term follow-up may involve monitoring for potential complications such as bile duct injury or development of post-cholecystectomy syndrome.
