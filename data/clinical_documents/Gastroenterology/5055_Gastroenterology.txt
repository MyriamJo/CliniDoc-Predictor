Medical Record:

Procedure: Colonoscopy with Tumor Localization

Reason for Procedure:

Positive fecal occult blood test (FOBT) and suspicion of colonic neoplasm.

Operative Details:

After obtaining informed consent, the patient was positioned appropriately for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was gently advanced through the rectum. Upon examination, a suspicious mass was identified in the sigmoid colon, characterized by an irregular, nodular surface and associated mucosal erythema.

Endoscopic tattooing was performed around the tumor site using sterile carbon particles to aid in localization for subsequent surgical resection. Multiple biopsies were obtained from the lesion for histopathological examination.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings suggestive of a colonic neoplasm in the sigmoid colon. Tumor localization achieved successfully with endoscopic tattooing for surgical planning.

Post-procedure Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of bleeding or perforation. Instructions were provided for post-biopsy care and monitoring for signs of complications. Surgical consultation scheduled for discussion of treatment options and planning for tumor resection. Follow-up appointment arranged for further management based on pathology results.
