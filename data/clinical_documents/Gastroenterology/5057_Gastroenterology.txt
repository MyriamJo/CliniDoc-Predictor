Medical Record:

Procedure: Colonoscopy with Colonic Diverticulosis Evaluation

Reason for Procedure:

Recurrent episodes of left lower abdominal pain and changes in bowel habits.

Operative Details:

Following informed consent, the patient was positioned appropriately for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was gently inserted through the rectum. The entire colon was carefully examined, revealing multiple outpouchings consistent with colonic diverticula, particularly prominent in the sigmoid colon.

Mucosal inspection showed evidence of mild inflammation and erythema around some diverticular openings. Biopsies were obtained from the affected areas to rule out any signs of diverticulitis or other pathological changes.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings consistent with colonic diverticulosis, with evidence of mild inflammation in some areas. Biopsies taken to rule out diverticulitis or other complications.

Post-procedure Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of perforation or bleeding. Instructions were provided for post-biopsy care and management of symptoms. Dietary and lifestyle recommendations were given to help manage diverticulosis. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of biopsy results and further management plan.
