INDICATIONS:
Evaluation of intermittent hemoptysis and chronic cough.

PROCEDURE DONE:
CT Pulmonary Angiography.

CT RESULTS:
Contrast-enhanced CT imaging of the thorax was performed to assess for pulmonary pathology. Findings reveal no evidence of pulmonary embolism within the pulmonary arteries. However, there are bilateral ground-glass opacities predominantly involving the lower lobes, along with areas of consolidation and bronchiectasis. Additionally, multiple subcentimeter pulmonary nodules are noted scattered throughout both lungs. No pleural effusion or significant lymphadenopathy observed.

CONCLUSIONS:

Negative CT pulmonary angiography for pulmonary embolism.
Bilateral ground-glass opacities, consolidation, and bronchiectasis suggestive of chronic inflammatory lung disease.
Multiple pulmonary nodules identified, warranting further characterization to determine etiology.
