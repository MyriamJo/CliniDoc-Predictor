Medical Record:

Procedure: Pediatric Colonoscopy with Biopsy

Reason for Procedure:

Chronic abdominal pain and persistent bloody stools in a pediatric patient.

Operative Details:

After obtaining parental consent and ensuring appropriate sedation, the pediatric patient was positioned comfortably for the procedure. The colonoscope was gently inserted through the rectum. The entire colon was meticulously examined, revealing diffuse inflammation, friability, and areas of ulceration, predominantly in the distal colon and rectum.

Multiple mucosal biopsies were obtained using cold forceps from affected areas for histopathological examination to ascertain the underlying cause of inflammation and to rule out conditions such as inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) or infectious colitis.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings suggestive of inflammatory colitis in the pediatric patient, with evidence of diffuse inflammation, ulceration, and friability. Biopsy samples obtained for further evaluation and diagnosis.

Post-procedure Care:

The pediatric patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of post-procedure complications. Instructions were provided to parents regarding post-biopsy care and management of symptoms. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of biopsy results and formulation of appropriate treatment plan tailored to the child's condition.
