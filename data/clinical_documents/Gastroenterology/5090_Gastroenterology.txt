Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with persistent rectal bleeding and concern for underlying colorectal pathology.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed using a lubricated anoscope, allowing for thorough examination of the anal canal and distal rectum. Visualization revealed evidence of multiple internal hemorrhoids with varying degrees of inflammation and bleeding. Careful inspection was conducted to identify any suspicious lesions or masses.

Impression:

Anoscopy findings consistent with internal hemorrhoids, with no evidence of other significant pathology noted.

Postoperative Care:

Following the procedure, the patient received comprehensive postoperative care instructions. This included dietary recommendations to maintain regular bowel habits and prevent constipation, which could exacerbate hemorrhoidal symptoms. Topical treatments, such as corticosteroid creams and suppositories, were prescribed to alleviate inflammation and discomfort. Sitz baths were recommended for additional relief and to promote healing.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to monitor symptom resolution and assess the effectiveness of conservative management strategies. Further interventions, such as rubber band ligation or infrared coagulation, would be considered if symptoms persisted or worsened.
