Medical Record:

Procedure: Colonoscopy with Biopsy

Reason for Procedure:

Chronic abdominal pain, unexplained weight loss, and intermittent rectal bleeding.

Operative Details:

Upon obtaining informed consent, the patient was positioned comfortably for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was inserted gently through the rectum. The entire colon was meticulously examined, revealing multiple ulcerative lesions with irregular borders and friable mucosa scattered throughout the descending and sigmoid colon.

Biopsies were obtained from representative areas of the lesions using cold forceps. Hemostasis was ensured at biopsy sites, and the colonoscope was carefully withdrawn.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings consistent with multifocal ulcerative colitis. Biopsy samples obtained for histopathological examination to confirm the diagnosis and assess disease severity.

Post-procedure Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of perforation or bleeding. Instructions were provided for post-biopsy care and management of symptoms. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of biopsy results and initiation of appropriate treatment plan.
