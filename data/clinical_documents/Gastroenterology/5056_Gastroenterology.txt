Medical Record:

Procedure: Colonoscopy with Hemorrhoid Banding

Reason for Procedure:

Chronic rectal bleeding and discomfort.

Operative Details:

After obtaining informed consent, the patient was positioned appropriately for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was gently inserted through the rectum. Upon examination, internal hemorrhoids were identified, characterized by enlarged vascular cushions protruding into the anal canal.

Hemorrhoid banding was performed using a specialized banding device, which applied rubber bands to the base of the hemorrhoids to cut off their blood supply and promote their shrinkage. Multiple hemorrhoids were banded during the procedure to address the patient's symptoms.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings consistent with internal hemorrhoids. Hemorrhoid banding performed successfully for symptom management.

Post-procedure Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of bleeding or discomfort. Instructions were provided for post-band ligation care, including dietary modifications and pain management. Follow-up appointment scheduled to assess treatment efficacy and further management as needed.
