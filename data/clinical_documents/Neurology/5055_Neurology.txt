Title of Procedure:
Electromyography-Guided Intramuscular Injection for Spasticity Management

Indication for Procedure:

A 45-year-old female presented with upper limb spasticity following a stroke. Clinical examination revealed increased muscle tone and involuntary muscle contractions affecting her right arm. Electromyography (EMG) was recommended to assess the severity of muscle spasticity and guide targeted treatment.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Right upper limb spasticity post-stroke.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Right upper limb spasticity post-stroke.

Procedure:

The patient was positioned comfortably in a sitting position. Following skin preparation, needle electrodes were inserted into the affected muscles to assess their electrical activity and degree of spasticity.

Under real-time EMG guidance, botulinum toxin was injected into the overactive muscles to induce localized chemical denervation and reduce spasticity. Multiple injection sites were targeted based on EMG findings, with careful consideration given to muscle size and distribution of spastic movements.

Impression:

EMG findings support the diagnosis of right upper limb spasticity post-stroke. Electromyography-guided intramuscular injection of botulinum toxin performed for targeted spasticity management.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was monitored for immediate side effects, such as temporary weakness or injection site discomfort. Rehabilitation therapy was initiated to optimize functional outcomes. Follow-up appointments were scheduled to assess treatment response and plan for further interventions as needed.
