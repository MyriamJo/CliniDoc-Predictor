Procedure:
Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with dysphagia and unintentional weight loss, raising concern for esophageal pathology.

Operative Details:

EGD was performed with the patient under conscious sedation. The endoscope was advanced orally into the esophagus, revealing a circumferential narrowing of the mid-esophagus with associated mucosal irregularities. Biopsies were obtained, and a dilation procedure was performed using a balloon dilator to relieve the stricture.

Impression:

EGD findings consistent with esophageal stricture requiring dilation for symptom relief.

Post-procedure Care:

Following dilation, the patient was instructed to follow a soft diet for a few days to allow the esophagus to heal. Proton pump inhibitors were prescribed to reduce acid reflux and prevent stricture recurrence. Close monitoring for signs of perforation or bleeding was undertaken in the immediate postoperative period.

Follow-up:

A repeat EGD was scheduled in several weeks to assess the effectiveness of dilation and monitor for stricture recurrence. Long-term surveillance for complications and underlying causes of the stricture, such as eosinophilic esophagitis or reflux disease, was planned.
