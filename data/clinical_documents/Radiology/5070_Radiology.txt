INDICATIONS:

Chronic lower back pain, history of lumbar disc herniation, and radiculopathy.

PROCEDURE DONE:

MRI Lumbar Spine without Contrast.

MRI RESULTS:

Multiplanar T1-weighted and T2-weighted sequences of the lumbar spine were obtained. No evidence of acute vertebral fractures or cord compression noted. Mild degenerative changes seen at L4-L5 and L5-S1 levels, with disc desiccation and mild foraminal stenosis.

CONCLUSIONS:

Degenerative changes noted at L4-L5 and L5-S1 levels consistent with known lumbar disc herniation.

No evidence of acute neural compression or significant spinal pathology requiring surgical intervention.
