INDICATIONS:

Progressive weakness, history of multiple sclerosis, and suspected spinal cord lesion.

PROCEDURE DONE:

MRI Cervical Spine with Contrast.

MRI RESULTS:

Contrast-enhanced multiplanar T1-weighted sequences of the cervical spine were obtained. No evidence of new enhancing lesions or active inflammation within the spinal cord or surrounding structures. Stable T2 hyperintensities noted in the cervical cord consistent with known demyelinating plaques.

CONCLUSIONS:

No evidence of acute spinal cord lesions or active demyelination on MRI.

Imaging findings consistent with stable multiple sclerosis without acute exacerbation.
