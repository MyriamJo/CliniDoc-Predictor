Procedure:
Barium Swallow

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with dysphagia and concern for possible esophageal obstruction or motility disorder.

Operative Details:

Barium swallow imaging was performed with the patient in a standing position. The patient ingested a barium sulfate suspension while radiographs were taken in multiple projections. Imaging revealed a narrowed segment in the mid-esophagus with delayed passage of barium, suggestive of an esophageal stricture.

Impression:

Barium swallow findings consistent with an esophageal stricture, warranting further evaluation for underlying etiology.

Post-procedure Care:

Following the procedure, the patient was advised to avoid solid foods until further evaluation could be completed. Referral to a gastroenterologist for endoscopic assessment and possible dilation was recommended. Symptomatic management with proton pump inhibitors and dietary modifications was initiated.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled with a gastroenterologist for endoscopic evaluation and therapeutic intervention if necessary. Long-term surveillance for stricture recurrence or complications was planned.
