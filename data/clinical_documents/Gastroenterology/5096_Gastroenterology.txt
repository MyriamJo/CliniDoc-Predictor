Procedure: 
Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with iron deficiency anemia and suspected gastrointestinal bleeding.

Operative Details:

EGD was performed with the patient under conscious sedation. Endoscopic examination revealed multiple linear erosions and areas of friability in the gastric body and fundus. No active bleeding was observed, but biopsies were obtained from abnormal areas for further evaluation.

Impression:

EGD findings consistent with gastritis and erosive changes in the gastric mucosa, warranting biopsy for histological assessment.

Post-procedure Care:

Following biopsy, the patient was started on iron supplementation to correct anemia. Proton pump inhibitors were prescribed to reduce gastric acid secretion and promote mucosal healing. Close monitoring for recurrence of anemia or gastrointestinal symptoms was advised.

Follow-up:

A follow-up EGD was scheduled to review biopsy results and assess treatment response. Management would be tailored based on histopathological findings and clinical outcomes. Long-term surveillance for recurrent bleeding or complications of gastritis would be undertaken.
