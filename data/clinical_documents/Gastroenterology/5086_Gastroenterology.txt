Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with complaints of rectal pain and occasional bright red blood in stool, suggesting potential hemorrhoidal issues or other anal pathologies.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed utilizing a lubricated anoscope, allowing direct visualization of the anal canal and lower rectum. Examination revealed evidence of internal hemorrhoids and minor mucosal tears, indicative of anal fissures. Gentle palpation was conducted to assess tenderness and identify any abnormalities.

Impression:

Anoscopy confirmed the presence of internal hemorrhoids and associated anal fissures.

Postoperative Care:

Post-procedure, the patient was provided with detailed instructions for postoperative care. Dietary recommendations, including increased fiber intake and adequate hydration, were advised to promote regular bowel movements and prevent constipation. Topical treatments such as hemorrhoidal creams and suppositories were prescribed for symptom relief. Additionally, sitz baths and proper anal hygiene practices were recommended.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to evaluate treatment response, address any lingering symptoms, and discuss further management options if needed.
