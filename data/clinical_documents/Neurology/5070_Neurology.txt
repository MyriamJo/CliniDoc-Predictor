Title of Procedure:
Video Electroencephalogram (EEG) Monitoring for Evaluation of Psychogenic Non-Epileptic Seizures (PNES)

Indication for Procedure:

A 30-year-old female presented with episodes of altered consciousness and motor symptoms resembling seizures. Clinical examination revealed no abnormal neurological findings between episodes, raising suspicion for psychogenic non-epileptic seizures (PNES). Video EEG monitoring was recommended to differentiate between epileptic and non-epileptic events and guide appropriate management.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Suspected psychogenic non-epileptic seizures.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Suspected psychogenic non-epileptic seizures.

Procedure:

The patient underwent placement of scalp electrodes according to the international 10-20 system, along with simultaneous video recording. The setup allowed for continuous monitoring of both clinical events and EEG activity, providing valuable information on the nature and timing of the episodes.

During the monitoring period, the patient's activities, behaviors, and any observed events were captured on video alongside EEG recordings. Provocative techniques, such as suggestion or exposure to triggering stimuli, may have been utilized to induce events and capture them on video.

Impression:

Video EEG monitoring revealed episodes of altered consciousness and motor behaviors characteristic of psychogenic non-epileptic seizures. EEG findings during these events were normal, without evidence of epileptiform activity, supporting the diagnosis of PNES.

Postoperative Care:

The patient's video EEG recordings were reviewed by a multidisciplinary team, including neurologists, psychiatrists, and psychologists, to confirm the diagnosis of PNES and develop an appropriate management plan. Counseling and psychoeducation were provided to address underlying psychological factors contributing to the seizures. Follow-up appointments were scheduled to monitor progress and adjust treatment strategies as needed.
