Title of Procedure:
Electromyography-Guided Motor Point Block for Chronic Myofascial Pain Syndrome

Indication for Procedure:

A 40-year-old female presented with chronic myofascial pain syndrome characterized by trigger points and muscle tenderness in the left trapezius muscle. Clinical examination revealed restricted range of motion and muscle spasms. Electromyography (EMG) was recommended to assess muscle involvement and guide targeted intervention.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Left trapezius myofascial pain syndrome.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Left trapezius myofascial pain syndrome.

Procedure:

The patient was positioned comfortably in a prone position. Following skin preparation, needle electrodes were inserted into the trapezius muscle to assess the electrical activity and identify motor points.

Under real-time EMG guidance, local anesthetic with or without corticosteroid was injected into the identified motor points to block nerve impulses and provide pain relief. The injection was administered using a fine-gauge needle to ensure accurate delivery to the targeted muscle fibers.

Procedure Details:

During the procedure, the patient was instructed to relax the affected muscle to facilitate precise needle placement and optimize medication distribution. Motor responses were monitored to confirm proper localization of the motor points and assess the efficacy of the injection.

Impression:

EMG findings support the diagnosis of left trapezius myofascial pain syndrome. Electromyography-guided motor point block performed for targeted pain relief.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was advised on post-procedural care, including activity modification and application of heat or ice packs to the injection site as needed. Follow-up appointments were scheduled to monitor pain relief and assess the need for additional interventions or rehabilitation.
