Medical Record:

Procedure: Carotid Endarterectomy (CEA)

Reason for Procedure:

Recurrent transient ischemic attacks (TIAs) and carotid bruit.

Operative Details:

After obtaining informed consent, the patient was positioned supine on the operating table. A combination of general anesthesia and local anesthetic infiltration was administered. The right common carotid artery was exposed through a longitudinal incision in the neck.

Upon exploration, a significant stenosis was noted in the right internal carotid artery. The artery was clamped proximally and distally to the stenosis. A longitudinal arteriotomy was performed, and the plaque was carefully dissected and removed. Thromboendarterectomy was meticulously conducted, ensuring complete removal of plaque material.

Following plaque removal, the arteriotomy site was closed using a patch graft harvested from the ipsilateral external carotid artery. The patch was secured in place with continuous 6-0 polypropylene sutures. Hemostasis was meticulously achieved, and the wound was irrigated with saline.

The wound was closed in layers, with the platysma and subcutaneous tissues approximated using absorbable sutures. Skin closure was performed using interrupted nylon sutures.

Impression:

Successful carotid endarterectomy with patch graft closure. No intraoperative complications observed.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was transferred to the recovery unit and monitored closely for neurological status and signs of complications. Antiplatelet therapy was initiated postoperatively as per protocol. Close follow-up was recommended for ongoing management of carotid artery disease.
