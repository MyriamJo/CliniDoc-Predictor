Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with anal pain and suspected anal fissure.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed using a lubricated anoscope. The anal canal and lower rectum were visualized, revealing a superficial anal fissure. The area was gently examined, and conservative management was recommended.

Impression:

Anoscopy revealed a superficial anal fissure, and conservative management was advised.

Postoperative Care:

Following the procedure, the patient was instructed to maintain proper anal hygiene and to avoid constipation. Sitz baths with warm water were recommended for pain relief and to promote healing. Additionally, dietary modifications to increase fiber intake and adequate hydration were advised to prevent straining during bowel movements.

Follow-up:

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment to monitor the healing progress of the anal fissure and to assess the effectiveness of conservative management. Further interventions, such as topical medications or minimally invasive procedures, would be considered based on the response to initial treatment.
