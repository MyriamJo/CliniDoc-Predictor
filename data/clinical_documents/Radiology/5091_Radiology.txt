INDICATIONS:
Assessment of chronic knee pain and suspected meniscal tear.

PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Knee without Contrast.

MRI RESULTS:
Multiplanar T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and proton density sequences of the knee joint were obtained. Findings reveal a complex horizontal tear involving the posterior horn of the medial meniscus with associated meniscal extrusion. Mild degenerative changes noted in the articular cartilage of the medial compartment. No evidence of ligamentous injury or significant effusion observed.

CONCLUSIONS:

Complex horizontal tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus with meniscal extrusion.
Mild degenerative changes in the articular cartilage of the medial compartment.
Absence of significant ligamentous injury or joint effusion.
