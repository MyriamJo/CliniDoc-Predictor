Title of Procedure:
Electromyography-Guided Peripheral Nerve Block for Chronic Radicular Pain

Indication for Procedure:

A 62-year-old male presented with chronic radicular pain radiating down his left leg, indicative of lumbar disc herniation. Clinical examination revealed reduced sensation and weakness in the affected lower limb. Electromyography (EMG) was recommended to confirm nerve involvement and guide targeted intervention.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Left lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Left lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy.

Procedure:

The patient was positioned comfortably in a prone position. Following skin preparation, needle electrodes were inserted along the distribution of the affected nerve roots. EMG was performed to assess the severity and distribution of nerve dysfunction.

Under real-time EMG guidance, a mixture of local anesthetic and corticosteroid was injected around the affected nerve roots to provide pain relief and reduce inflammation. Careful attention was paid to target the specific nerve roots identified by EMG as contributing to the patient's symptoms.

Impression:

EMG findings support the diagnosis of left lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy. Electromyography-guided peripheral nerve block performed for targeted pain relief.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was advised on post-procedural care, including activity modification and potential temporary exacerbation of symptoms. Follow-up appointments were scheduled to monitor pain relief and assess the need for further interventions.
