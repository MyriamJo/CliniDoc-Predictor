EXAM:
X-ray Hand.
REASON FOR EXAM:
Assessment of hand pain following trauma.

TECHNIQUE:
Posteroanterior and lateral views of the hand are obtained.

FINDINGS:
No evidence of acute fractures or dislocations identified. Soft tissue swelling noted around the metacarpophalangeal joints. No foreign bodies appreciated. Bony structures appear intact.

IMPRESSION:
Negative X-ray findings for acute fractures or dislocations. Soft tissue swelling noted, likely secondary to trauma. Clinical correlation recommended for further evaluation of hand pain.
