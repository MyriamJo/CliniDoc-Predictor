EXAM:
MRI Lumbar Spine without Contrast.

REASON FOR EXAM:

Evaluation of chronic low back pain and radiculopathy.

TECHNIQUE:

Sagittal and axial T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and STIR sequences of the lumbar spine are obtained.

FINDINGS:

Mild disc desiccation and height loss at L4-L5 and L5-S1 levels. Mild bilateral facet arthropathy. No evidence of spinal canal stenosis or significant neural foraminal narrowing. No acute compression fractures. Cord signal intensity is within normal limits.

IMPRESSION:

Degenerative changes of the lumbar spine without acute findings. Mild disc degeneration at L4-L5 and L5-S1 levels. Clinical correlation recommended for management of chronic low back pain and radiculopathy.
