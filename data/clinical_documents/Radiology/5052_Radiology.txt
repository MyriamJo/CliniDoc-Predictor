EXAM:
CT Abdomen and Pelvis with Contrast.

REASON FOR EXAM:

Evaluation of abdominal pain and suspected renal stone.

TECHNIQUE:

Axial images of the abdomen and pelvis are obtained before and after intravenous administration of contrast material.

FINDINGS:

The liver, spleen, pancreas, and adrenal glands demonstrate normal enhancement patterns. No evidence of intra-abdominal masses or fluid collections. Both kidneys are visualized, and there is no evidence of hydronephrosis. No ureteral stones identified. Bowel loops appear unremarkable. No evidence of lymphadenopathy.

IMPRESSION:

Normal CT abdomen and pelvis without acute findings. Rule out non-obstructing renal stone. Clinical correlation recommended for further evaluation of abdominal pain.
