Indications: 
Evaluation of suspected intra-articular knee pathology, including meniscal tear.
Procedure Done: 
MRI Knee with Intra-Articular Gadolinium Contrast Injection.
MRI Results:
MRI imaging of the knee joint was performed following intra-articular injection of gadolinium-based contrast material. Multiplanar T1-weighted and proton density sequences were obtained. Findings reveal a complex tear involving the posterior horn of the medial meniscus with associated meniscal extrusion and subchondral bone marrow edema. Mild joint effusion noted without evidence of ligamentous injury or significant cartilage defects.
Conclusions:
MRI findings consistent with complex tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus with meniscal extrusion.
Mild joint effusion noted without evidence of ligamentous injury or significant cartilage defects.
