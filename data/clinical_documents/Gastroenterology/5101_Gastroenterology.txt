Procedure:
Barium Swallow

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with intermittent chest pain and suspected esophageal dysmotility.

Operative Details:

Barium swallow imaging was performed with the patient in an upright position. The patient ingested a barium sulfate suspension, and dynamic fluoroscopic images were obtained during swallowing. Imaging revealed evidence of esophageal dysmotility with abnormal peristalsis and delayed passage of barium through the esophagus.

Impression:

Barium swallow findings consistent with esophageal dysmotility, suggestive of underlying motility disorder or neuromuscular dysfunction.

Post-procedure Care:

Following the procedure, the patient was referred to a gastroenterologist for further evaluation with esophageal manometry and pH monitoring. Symptomatic management with prokinetic agents or neuromodulators may be considered based on diagnostic findings. Dietary modifications, such as small frequent meals and avoiding large bolus intake, were recommended to minimize dysphagia symptoms.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to review diagnostic test results and discuss treatment options for esophageal dysmotility. Long-term management would focus on symptom control and prevention of complications related to dysphagia.
