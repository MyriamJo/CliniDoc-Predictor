Medical Record:

Procedure: Colonoscopy with Polypectomy

Reason for Procedure:

Positive fecal occult blood test (FOBT) and family history of colorectal cancer.

Operative Details:

After obtaining informed consent, the patient was positioned appropriately for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was advanced carefully through the rectum. The entire colon was meticulously examined, revealing multiple polyps ranging in size from 3 to 10 millimeters scattered throughout the colon, predominantly in the sigmoid and descending colon.

Polypectomy was performed using cold snare technique for smaller polyps and hot snare technique for larger polyps. Hemostasis was ensured at polypectomy sites, and the colonoscope was slowly withdrawn.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings consistent with multiple colonic polyps, predominantly in the sigmoid and descending colon. Polypectomy performed successfully during the procedure.

Post-procedure Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of bleeding or perforation. Instructions were provided for post-polypectomy care and monitoring for signs of complications. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of pathology results and further management plan, including surveillance colonoscopy interval.
