INDICATIONS:
Evaluation of suspected acute stroke.

PROCEDURE DONE:
CT Head without Contrast.

CT RESULTS:
Non-contrast CT imaging of the head was performed. Findings reveal a hyperdense MCA sign in the right middle cerebral artery (MCA) consistent with acute thromboembolic occlusion. Hypodensity observed in the right basal ganglia and insular cortex indicative of acute infarction. No evidence of intracranial hemorrhage or midline shift noted.

CONCLUSIONS:

Hyperdense MCA sign in the right MCA suggestive of acute thromboembolic occlusion.
Acute infarction involving the right basal ganglia and insular cortex.
Absence of intracranial hemorrhage or midline shift.
