Medical Record:

Procedure: Adolescent Colonoscopy with Polyp Surveillance

Reason for Procedure:

History of juvenile polyposis syndrome and routine surveillance in an adolescent patient.

Operative Details:

After obtaining informed consent from the adolescent patient and their guardian, the patient was positioned comfortably for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was gently inserted through the rectum. The entire colon was meticulously examined, revealing multiple juvenile polyps scattered throughout the colon, with a higher concentration observed in the rectum and sigmoid colon.

Polypectomy was performed using a combination of cold snare and hot snare techniques, targeting larger polyps while ensuring complete removal and minimal trauma to the surrounding mucosa. Careful inspection and removal of all visible polyps were undertaken during the procedure.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings consistent with juvenile polyposis syndrome in the adolescent patient, characterized by multiple juvenile polyps scattered throughout the colon, predominantly in the rectum and sigmoid colon. Polypectomy performed successfully during the procedure.

Post-procedure Care:

The adolescent patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of post-polypectomy complications. Instructions were provided regarding post-procedure care, including dietary recommendations and monitoring for signs of bleeding or perforation. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of pathology results and planning of future surveillance colonoscopies tailored to the patient's condition.
