INDICATIONS:
Assessment of suspected renal artery stenosis in a patient with refractory hypertension.
PROCEDURE DONE:
CT Renal Angiography.

CT RESULTS:
Contrast-enhanced CT imaging of the renal arteries was performed. Findings reveal mild atherosclerotic changes with focal luminal irregularities in the proximal right renal artery. No evidence of hemodynamically significant stenosis (>50%) or renal artery occlusion noted. Renal perfusion appears symmetric, with preserved enhancement of renal parenchyma bilaterally.

CONCLUSIONS:

Mild atherosclerotic changes with focal luminal irregularities in the proximal right renal artery.
No evidence of hemodynamically significant renal artery stenosis or occlusion.
Symmetric renal perfusion with preserved enhancement of renal parenchyma.
