Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with pruritus ani and suspected perianal dermatitis.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed using a disposable anoscope. The anal canal and perianal area were examined, revealing signs of inflammation consistent with perianal dermatitis. Topical corticosteroid cream was prescribed for symptomatic relief.

Impression:

Anoscopy confirmed the diagnosis of perianal dermatitis, and topical treatment was initiated.

Postoperative Care:

After the procedure, the patient received instructions to maintain good perianal hygiene and to avoid potential irritants such as perfumed soaps. The application of prescribed topical corticosteroid cream was advised according to the healthcare provider's directions. Additionally, the patient was advised to follow up if symptoms persisted or worsened despite treatment.

Follow-up:

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment to evaluate the response to treatment, assess for any adverse reactions to medication, and make further management decisions as necessary.
