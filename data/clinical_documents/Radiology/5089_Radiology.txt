INDICATIONS:
Evaluation of intermittent chest pain, dyspnea on exertion, and history of tobacco use.

PROCEDURE DONE:
CT Angiography of the Chest with High-Resolution Lung Imaging.

CT RESULTS:

Lung Imaging:
High-resolution imaging of the lungs was performed to assess parenchymal abnormalities. Findings reveal bilateral diffuse emphysematous changes with predominantly apical and subpleural distribution. Additionally, there are scattered areas of ground-glass opacities, suggestive of underlying interstitial lung disease. No definite pulmonary nodules or masses identified. Mild bronchiectasis noted in the right middle lobe.

Pulmonary Vasculature:
Contrast-enhanced imaging of the pulmonary arteries was performed to evaluate for pulmonary embolism. No evidence of acute pulmonary embolism or filling defects within the pulmonary vasculature observed. Pulmonary arterial anatomy appears normal without evidence of significant stenosis or aneurysmal dilatation.

Thoracic Aorta:
Assessment of the thoracic aorta reveals normal caliber and morphology without evidence of aortic dissection or aneurysmal dilatation. No intramural hematoma or atherosclerotic plaque noted along the aortic wall.

CONCLUSIONS:

Bilateral diffuse emphysematous changes with apical and subpleural predominance, indicative of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
Scattered ground-glass opacities suggestive of interstitial lung disease, warranting further evaluation for etiology.
Absence of acute pulmonary embolism or significant pulmonary vascular abnormalities.
Normal thoracic aorta without evidence of dissection or aneurysm.
