Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with rectal bleeding and suspected perianal abscess.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed using a lubricated anoscope to evaluate the anal canal and surrounding tissues. Examination revealed a tender fluctuant mass consistent with a perianal abscess. Immediate incision and drainage were performed under local anesthesia, followed by thorough irrigation of the abscess cavity.

Impression:

Anoscopy confirmed the presence of a perianal abscess requiring surgical intervention.

Postoperative Care:

Following incision and drainage, the patient received post-procedural care instructions. This included wound care management, such as sitz baths and application of topical antibiotics, to promote healing and prevent infection. Analgesics were prescribed for pain relief, and close monitoring for signs of complications, such as recurrence or abscess formation, was advised.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to assess wound healing progress and ensure resolution of symptoms. Further management options, including preventive measures to reduce the risk of recurrent abscesses, would be discussed based on clinical outcomes and patient preferences.
