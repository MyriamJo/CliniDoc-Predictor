Title of Procedure: 
Electromyography-Guided Percutaneous Disc Decompression for Lumbar Disc Herniation

Indication for Procedure:

A 45-year-old male presented with severe lower back pain radiating down his left leg, accompanied by numbness and weakness. Clinical examination revealed diminished deep tendon reflexes and positive straight leg raise test. Electromyography (EMG) was recommended to confirm nerve involvement and guide targeted intervention.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Left L5-S1 lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Left L5-S1 lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy.

Procedure:

The patient was positioned prone on the fluoroscopy table. Following sterile preparation and local anesthesia, a needle electrode was inserted under EMG guidance into the affected lumbar paraspinal muscles to assess the electrical activity and identify the precise location of nerve compression.

Under real-time EMG guidance, a specialized cannula was advanced to the site of the herniated disc. Using a percutaneous approach, a radiofrequency probe or laser fiber was introduced through the cannula to ablate or dissolve a portion of the herniated disc material, relieving pressure on the compressed nerve roots.

Impression:

EMG findings support the diagnosis of left L5-S1 lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy. Electromyography-guided percutaneous disc decompression performed for targeted relief of nerve compression.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was monitored post-procedure for immediate relief of symptoms and potential complications. Instructions were given regarding activity modification and post-procedure rehabilitation. Follow-up appointments were scheduled to assess the long-term effectiveness of the procedure and address any residual symptoms.
