EXAM:
X-ray Shoulder.

REASON FOR EXAM:

Evaluation of shoulder pain and limited range of motion.

TECHNIQUE:

Anteroposterior and lateral views of the right shoulder are obtained.

FINDINGS:

No evidence of acute fracture or dislocation. No osseous abnormalities identified. Glenohumeral joint space is preserved. Acromioclavicular joint is normal. Soft tissues appear unremarkable without evidence of calcific tendinitis or significant effusion.

IMPRESSION:

Normal X-ray of the right shoulder without acute bony abnormalities. Clinical correlation recommended for further evaluation of shoulder pain and limited range of motion.
