Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with rectal bleeding and suspected hemorrhoids.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed using a disposable anoscope. The anoscope was gently inserted into the anal canal to visualize the lower rectum and anal canal. Examination revealed internal hemorrhoids, which were treated with infrared coagulation.

Impression:

Successful anoscopy performed to evaluate rectal bleeding and treat internal hemorrhoids.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was advised to maintain good anal hygiene and avoid straining during bowel movements. Topical analgesics were prescribed for pain relief, and instructions were given regarding dietary modifications to prevent constipation.

Follow-up:

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment to assess treatment response and evaluate for any recurrence of symptoms.
