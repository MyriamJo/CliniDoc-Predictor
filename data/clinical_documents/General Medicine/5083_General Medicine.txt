Chief Complaint:
The patient presents with unexplained weight loss and fatigue.

History of Present Illness:
A 70-year-old male reports unintentional weight loss of approximately 15 pounds over the past three months. He notes decreased appetite and feelings of weakness and fatigue, which have progressively worsened. He denies any gastrointestinal symptoms, fever, or night sweats. Additionally, he reports increased thirst and frequency of urination over the same period. He has no history of diabetes mellitus or thyroid disorders.

Past Medical History:
Hypertension managed with antihypertensive medications.

Family History:
Negative for endocrine disorders.

Social History:
The patient leads a sedentary lifestyle, retired from work. He denies smoking or alcohol consumption.

Review of Systems:
Negative for fever, rash, or other constitutional symptoms. No gastrointestinal or respiratory symptoms reported.

Physical Examination:
Vital Signs: Within normal limits.
Endocrine: No palpable thyroid nodules. No signs of Cushing's syndrome or acromegaly appreciated. No peripheral edema noted.

Assessment:

Unexplained Weight Loss: Concerning for underlying systemic illness or endocrine disorder such as hyperthyroidism or diabetes mellitus.
Polyuria and Polydipsia: Suggestive of possible diabetes mellitus or diabetes insipidus.
Plan:

Laboratory Investigations: 
Order fasting blood glucose, glycosylated hemoglobin (HbA1c), thyroid function tests, and comprehensive metabolic panel to evaluate for diabetes mellitus and other metabolic abnormalities.

Urinalysis:
Perform urinalysis to assess for glucosuria and other urinary abnormalities.

Endocrine Consultation: 
Referral to an endocrinologist for further evaluation and management of suspected endocrine disorders.

Symptomatic Treatment: 
Provide supportive measures for fatigue and weakness, including dietary counseling and nutritional supplements if necessary.

Follow-up: 
Schedule a follow-up appointment in two weeks to review laboratory results and discuss further management options based on findings.
