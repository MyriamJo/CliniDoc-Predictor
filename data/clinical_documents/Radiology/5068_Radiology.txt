INDICATIONS:

Chronic abdominal pain, history of gallstones, and dyspepsia.

PROCEDURE DONE:

Hepatobiliary Scintigraphy (HIDA scan).

STRESS ECG RESULTS:

The patient underwent infusion of CCK (cholecystokinin) at a rate of 0.02 μg/kg/min for 60 minutes following a 60-minute fasting period. The resting electrocardiogram shows a regular sinus rhythm with a heart rate of 78 beats per minute, no significant ST segment abnormalities noted. The heart rate remained relatively stable throughout the procedure. Blood pressure was within normal limits.

HIDA SCAN RESULTS:

Hepatobiliary scintigraphy was performed using 6 mCi of Tc-99m mebrofenin. There was prompt visualization of the liver and no evidence of biliary obstruction. No evidence of delayed gallbladder visualization suggestive of cholecystitis. Physiologic excretion of tracer into the small bowel was noted.

CONCLUSIONS:

Negative HIDA scan for acute cholecystitis or biliary obstruction.

Physiologic hepatobiliary excretion pattern observed, indicating normal hepatobiliary function.
