Medical Record:

Procedure: Colonoscopy with Mucosal Biopsy

Reason for Procedure:

Chronic diarrhea, abdominal cramping, and unintended weight loss.

Operative Details:

Following informed consent, the patient was positioned appropriately for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was gently inserted through the rectum. The entire colon was meticulously examined, revealing diffuse mucosal erythema, granularity, and loss of vascular pattern extending from the rectum to the ascending colon.

Multiple mucosal biopsies were obtained using cold forceps from affected areas along the colon. Hemostasis was ensured, and the colonoscope was gradually withdrawn.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings consistent with diffuse colonic inflammation suggestive of inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), such as ulcerative colitis or Crohn's disease. Biopsy samples obtained for histopathological evaluation to confirm the diagnosis and assess disease severity.

Post-procedure Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of perforation or bleeding. Instructions were provided for post-biopsy care and management of symptoms. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of biopsy results and initiation of appropriate treatment plan for IBD.
