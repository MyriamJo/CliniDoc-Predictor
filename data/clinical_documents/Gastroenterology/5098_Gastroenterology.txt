Procedure:
Barium Swallow

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with chronic reflux symptoms and suspected gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD).

Operative Details:

Barium swallow imaging was performed with the patient in a supine position. The patient ingested a barium sulfate suspension, and dynamic fluoroscopic images were obtained during swallowing. Imaging revealed evidence of gastroesophageal reflux with retrograde flow of barium into the distal esophagus and evidence of esophageal mucosal irritation.

Impression:

Barium swallow findings consistent with gastroesophageal reflux and evidence of esophagitis.

Post-procedure Care:

Following the procedure, the patient was started on proton pump inhibitor therapy to reduce gastric acid secretion and alleviate symptoms. Lifestyle modifications, including dietary changes and elevation of the head of the bed, were recommended to minimize reflux. Close monitoring for symptom improvement and medication side effects was advised.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to assess treatment response and adjust management as needed. Long-term management would focus on symptom control, prevention of complications such as Barrett's esophagus, and surveillance for disease progression.
