Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with chronic anal discomfort and suspected anal fissures.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed using a disposable anoscope, allowing for direct visualization of the anal canal. Examination revealed evidence of anal fissures with associated erythema and tenderness upon palpation. Gentle pressure was applied to assess for bleeding and to evaluate the extent of the fissures.

Impression:

Anoscopy confirmed the presence of anal fissures with associated inflammation.

Postoperative Care:

In the postoperative period, the patient received detailed instructions for care. This included dietary modifications to ensure soft stools and prevent constipation, thereby promoting healing of the fissures. Topical analgesics and barrier creams were prescribed to alleviate pain and protect the affected area. Adequate hydration and fiber intake were encouraged to maintain bowel regularity.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to monitor the healing progress of the anal fissures and assess symptom resolution. Further interventions, such as sphincterotomy or botulinum toxin injections, would be considered based on treatment response and clinical need.
