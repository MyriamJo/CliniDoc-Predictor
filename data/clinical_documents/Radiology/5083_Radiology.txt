INDICATIONS:
Chronic knee pain, instability, and suspected ligamentous injury.
PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Knee without Contrast.

MRI RESULTS:
Multiplanar T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and proton density sequences of the knee joint were obtained. Findings reveal a moderate-grade sprain of the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) with associated bone marrow edema along the lateral femoral condyle. Additionally, mild joint effusion is observed. Notably, there is no evidence of meniscal tear or significant cartilage defects.

CONCLUSIONS:

Moderate-grade sprain of the ACL with associated bone marrow edema.
Absence of meniscal tear or significant cartilage pathology.
Mild joint effusion indicative of ongoing inflammation.
