Title of Procedure: 
Long-Term Video Electroencephalogram (EEG) Monitoring for Evaluation of Refractory Epilepsy

Indication for Procedure:

A 30-year-old female with a known history of epilepsy presented with ongoing seizures despite treatment with multiple antiepileptic medications. Clinical examination revealed no focal neurological deficits. Long-term video electroencephalogram (EEG) monitoring was recommended to capture and characterize seizure semiology and guide further management.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Refractory epilepsy.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Refractory epilepsy.

Procedure:

The patient underwent placement of scalp electrodes according to the international 10-20 system, along with simultaneous video recording. The setup allowed for continuous monitoring of both clinical events and EEG activity over several days or weeks.

During the monitoring period, the patient's activities and behaviors were captured on video alongside EEG recordings. The patient was encouraged to maintain normal activities to provoke seizures and capture them on video.

Impression:

Long-term video EEG monitoring captured multiple seizure events characterized by focal motor seizures with preserved awareness followed by secondary generalization. EEG findings demonstrated interictal epileptiform discharges localized to the left frontal lobe.

Postoperative Care:

The patient's video EEG recordings were reviewed by a multidisciplinary team, including epileptologists and neurophysiologists, to characterize the seizures and guide treatment decisions. Counseling was provided regarding seizure safety measures and lifestyle modifications. Treatment options, including optimization of medications or consideration of surgical evaluation, were discussed during follow-up appointments.
