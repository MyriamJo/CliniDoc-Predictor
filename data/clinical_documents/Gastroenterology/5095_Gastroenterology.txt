Procedure: 
Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with persistent nausea and vomiting, concerning for gastric outlet obstruction.

Operative Details:

EGD was performed with the patient under conscious sedation. Endoscopic examination revealed a large mass causing near-complete luminal obstruction in the gastric antrum. Multiple biopsies were obtained, and a stent was placed endoscopically to relieve the obstruction and improve gastric emptying.

Impression:

EGD findings consistent with gastric outlet obstruction secondary to a mass lesion, likely of neoplastic origin.

Post-procedure Care:

Following stent placement, the patient was started on a liquid diet to facilitate passage of food through the stent. Prophylactic antibiotics were administered to reduce the risk of stent-related complications such as infection. Close monitoring for stent migration or occlusion was performed.

Follow-up:

A follow-up EGD was scheduled to assess stent patency and tumor response to neoadjuvant therapy if indicated. Long-term management would involve multidisciplinary collaboration with oncologists for further treatment planning.
