Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient sought evaluation for persistent anal discomfort and suspected perianal abscess.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed utilizing a disposable anoscope. The examination revealed a tender fluctuant mass suggestive of a perianal abscess. Immediate incision and drainage were conducted under local anesthesia, followed by thorough irrigation of the abscess cavity.

Impression:

Anoscopy confirmed the presence of a perianal abscess necessitating incision and drainage for symptomatic relief.

Postoperative Care:

The patient received post-procedural wound care instructions, including regular sitz baths and application of topical antibiotic ointment. Oral antibiotics were prescribed to prevent infection and promote healing. Pain management was provided as needed.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to monitor wound healing, assess for recurrence, and address any concerns or complications.
