Procedure:

Colonoscopy
Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with chronic diarrhea, unintentional weight loss, and a family history of colorectal cancer.

Operative Details:

Colonoscopy was performed with the patient under conscious sedation. The colonoscope was advanced to the cecum, allowing for a thorough examination of the entire colon. Multiple polyps were identified in the descending colon and rectum. Polypectomy was performed for each lesion, and the specimens were sent for histopathological evaluation.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings included multiple colonic polyps, which were successfully removed for further analysis to rule out malignancy or dysplasia.

Post-procedure Care:

Post-polypectomy, the patient was instructed to follow a low-residue diet to minimize bowel irritation and to avoid aspirin or NSAIDs to reduce the risk of bleeding. The patient was advised to report any signs of significant abdominal pain, rectal bleeding, or fever immediately. Adequate hydration was emphasized to aid recovery.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to discuss the histopathological findings of the resected polyps. Depending on the results, further management may include increased surveillance colonoscopies or additional therapeutic interventions. The patient was also referred to a genetic counselor for evaluation due to the family history of colorectal cancer.
