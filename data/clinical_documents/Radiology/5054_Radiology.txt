Ultrasound Thyroid.

REASON FOR EXAM:

Evaluation of palpable thyroid nodule.

TECHNIQUE:

Transverse and longitudinal grayscale and Doppler imaging of the thyroid gland is performed.

FINDINGS:

A 1.5 cm hypoechoic nodule is identified in the right lobe of the thyroid gland. No suspicious features such as microcalcifications or irregular margins are noted. Normal vascularity on Doppler imaging. The left thyroid lobe appears unremarkable. No cervical lymphadenopathy detected.

IMPRESSION:

Solitary thyroid nodule in the right lobe without concerning features. Recommend clinical correlation and consideration of fine-needle aspiration biopsy for further evaluation.
