Indications: 
Chronic right shoulder pain, limited range of motion, and history of trauma.
Procedure Done: MRI Shoulder with Arthrogram.
MRI Results:
Contrast-enhanced MRI of the right shoulder was performed using intra-articular injection of gadolinium-based contrast material. Multiplanar T1-weighted and T2-weighted sequences were obtained. Findings reveal a full-thickness tear of the supraspinatus tendon with retraction. Additionally, there is evidence of moderate subacromial-subdeltoid bursitis. No evidence of labral tear or significant glenohumeral joint abnormalities noted.
Conclusions:
Full-thickness tear of the supraspinatus tendon with retraction.
Moderate subacromial-subdeltoid bursitis.
No evidence of significant glenohumeral joint pathology requiring surgical intervention.
