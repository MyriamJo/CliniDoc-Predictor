Medical Record:

Procedure: Colonoscopy with Polyp Surveillance

Reason for Procedure:

Previous history of adenomatous polyps and routine surveillance.

Operative Details:

After obtaining informed consent, the patient was positioned appropriately for the procedure. Conscious sedation was administered, and the colonoscope was inserted gently through the rectum. The entire colon was meticulously examined, revealing multiple small adenomatous polyps scattered throughout the colon, particularly in the transverse and descending colon.

Polypectomy was performed using cold snare technique for smaller polyps and hot snare technique for larger polyps. Additionally, chromoendoscopy with indigo carmine dye was utilized to enhance visualization of subtle polyps.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings consistent with multiple adenomatous polyps, in line with the patient's previous history. Polypectomy performed successfully during the procedure.

Post-procedure Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of bleeding or perforation. Instructions were provided for post-polypectomy care and monitoring for signs of complications. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of pathology results and planning of future surveillance colonoscopies.
