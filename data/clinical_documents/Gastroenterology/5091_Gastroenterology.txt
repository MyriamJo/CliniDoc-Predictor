Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with chronic anal pain and suspected anal fistula.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was performed using a lubricated anoscope, allowing for direct visualization of the anal canal and perianal region. Examination revealed a small external opening with surrounding inflammation, suggestive of a possible anal fistula. Gentle probing was performed to assess the extent and direction of the fistulous tract.

Impression:

Anoscopy findings consistent with a simple anal fistula, likely of cryptoglandular origin.

Postoperative Care:

Post-procedurally, the patient received detailed instructions for wound care and symptom management. This included warm sitz baths and application of topical antibiotics to promote healing and prevent infection. Pain management was provided with oral analgesics as needed, and instructions were given to avoid activities that may exacerbate symptoms.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to monitor wound healing progress and assess for resolution of symptoms. Further evaluation, including imaging studies such as MRI or endoanal ultrasound, may be considered if symptoms persist or if complications arise. Long-term management options, including surgical intervention if necessary, would be discussed based on clinical outcomes and patient preferences.
