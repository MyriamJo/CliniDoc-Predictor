EXAM:
DEXA Scan (Dual-Energy X-ray Absorptiometry).

REASON FOR EXAM:

Assessment of bone mineral density for osteoporosis screening.

TECHNIQUE:

Measurements of bone density at the lumbar spine and proximal femur are obtained using dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry.

FINDINGS:

T-score of -2.5 at the lumbar spine, indicating osteoporosis. T-score of -2.0 at the left femoral neck and -1.8 at the total left hip, indicating osteopenia. No significant vertebral fractures identified.

IMPRESSION:

Osteoporosis at the lumbar spine and osteopenia at the left femoral neck and total left hip. Recommend initiation of appropriate management and lifestyle modifications for osteoporosis prevention.
