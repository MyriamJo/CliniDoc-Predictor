Procedure: Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with obstructive jaundice and right upper quadrant pain, suggestive of choledocholithiasis.

Operative Details:

An urgent open cholecystectomy with common bile duct exploration was performed. Multiple stones were extracted from the common bile duct, and the gallbladder was meticulously dissected and removed.

Impression:

Successful open cholecystectomy with common bile duct exploration for choledocholithiasis, with resolution of obstructive jaundice.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of postoperative complications such as bleeding, infection, or bile leakage. Pain management and intravenous fluids were administered as needed to ensure comfort and hydration.

Instructions were provided for postoperative care, including gradual advancement of diet and avoidance of heavy lifting or strenuous activity during the initial recovery period.

Follow-up:

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment with the surgical team to assess postoperative progress, review pathology results, and address any concerns or questions regarding the procedure. Long-term follow-up may involve monitoring for potential complications such as bile duct stricture or recurrent stones.
