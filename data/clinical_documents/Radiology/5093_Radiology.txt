INDICATIONS:
Evaluation of chronic lower back pain and radiculopathy.

PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Lumbar Spine without Contrast.

MRI RESULTS:
Multiplanar T1-weighted and T2-weighted sequences of the lumbar spine were obtained. Findings reveal mild degenerative disc disease with disc desiccation and posterior disc bulging at the L4-L5 and L5-S1 levels. No evidence of significant central canal or neural foraminal stenosis noted. Mild facet arthropathy observed at multiple levels.

CONCLUSIONS:

Mild degenerative disc disease with disc desiccation and posterior disc bulging at the L4-L5 and L5-S1 levels.
Mild facet arthropathy at multiple lumbar levels.
No significant central canal or neural foraminal stenosis.
