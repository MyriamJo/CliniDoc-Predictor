Medical Record:

Procedure: Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)

Reason for Procedure:

Recurrent episodes of severe upper abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting, suggestive of acute cholecystitis with suspected gallstones.

Operative Details:

The patient underwent cholecystectomy, a surgical procedure to remove the gallbladder, performed under general anesthesia. The operation was conducted using a laparoscopic approach, involving several small incisions in the abdominal wall.

Once access was gained, the surgeon carefully identified the gallbladder and surrounding structures. Dissection was performed to free the gallbladder from its attachments to the liver and surrounding tissues. The cystic duct and artery were ligated and divided to isolate the gallbladder.

The gallbladder was then extracted through one of the small incisions, and meticulous attention was paid to ensure complete removal without any spillage of bile or stones. Hemostasis was achieved, and the incisions were closed with sutures or surgical staples.

Impression:

Successful laparoscopic cholecystectomy performed without intraoperative complications. The procedure was indicated due to recurrent episodes of acute cholecystitis and suspected gallstones, aiming to alleviate symptoms and prevent future complications.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was closely monitored in the recovery area for any signs of postoperative complications such as bleeding, infection, or bile leakage. Pain management and intravenous fluids were administered as needed to ensure comfort and hydration.

Instructions were provided for postoperative care, including gradual advancement of diet and avoidance of heavy lifting or strenuous activity during the initial recovery period.

Follow-up:

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment with the surgical team to assess postoperative progress, review pathology results, and address any concerns or questions regarding the procedure. Long-term follow-up may involve monitoring for potential complications such as bile duct injury or development of post-cholecystectomy syndrome.
