EXAM:
MRI Cervical Spine with Contrast.

REASON FOR EXAM:

Evaluation of neck pain and radiculopathy.

TECHNIQUE:

Sagittal and axial T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and post-contrast T1-weighted images of the cervical spine are obtained.

FINDINGS:

No evidence of acute disc herniation or significant spinal cord compression. Mild disc degeneration at multiple levels without significant neural foraminal narrowing. No evidence of enhancing masses or abnormal cord signal intensity. Paraspinal soft tissues appear unremarkable.

IMPRESSION:

Mild degenerative changes of the cervical spine without acute findings. No evidence of significant cord compression or enhancing lesions. Clinical correlation recommended for management of neck pain and radiculopathy.
