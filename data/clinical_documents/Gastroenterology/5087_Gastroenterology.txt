Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with chronic rectal itching and discomfort, raising suspicion for perianal dermatological conditions or other underlying issues.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was conducted using a disposable anoscope, allowing visualization of the anal canal and surrounding tissues. Examination revealed evidence of perianal erythema and superficial excoriations suggestive of dermatitis. Careful inspection was performed to rule out other potential etiologies.

Impression:

Anoscopy findings consistent with perianal dermatitis.

Postoperative Care:

Following the procedure, the patient was provided with postoperative care instructions. This included recommendations for gentle cleansing with mild soap and water, followed by the application of topical corticosteroid creams to reduce inflammation and itching. Emollient ointments were also prescribed to maintain skin hydration and integrity.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was arranged to assess treatment response, monitor for any recurrence of symptoms, and discuss long-term management strategies, including lifestyle modifications and ongoing skincare practices.
