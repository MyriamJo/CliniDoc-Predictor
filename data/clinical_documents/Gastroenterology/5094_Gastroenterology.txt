Procedure: 
Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with chronic abdominal pain and suspected peptic ulcer disease.

Operative Details:

EGD was performed with the patient under conscious sedation. Endoscopic examination of the stomach revealed a well-defined ulcer crater with surrounding erythema in the gastric antrum. Biopsies were obtained from the ulcer base, and proton pump inhibitor therapy was initiated.

Impression:

EGD findings consistent with a gastric ulcer, warranting biopsy for histological evaluation.

Post-procedure Care:

Following biopsy, the patient was advised to adhere to a bland diet and avoid nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) or aspirin to prevent ulcer exacerbation. Proton pump inhibitor therapy was continued for ulcer healing. Close monitoring for bleeding or perforation was conducted.

Follow-up:

A follow-up EGD was scheduled to assess ulcer healing and biopsy results. Management would be tailored based on histopathological findings and patient response to therapy.
