INDICATIONS:
Acute abdominal pain, suspicion of acute appendicitis.
PROCEDURE DONE:
CT Abdomen and Pelvis with Oral and Intravenous Contrast.

CT RESULTS:
Contrast-enhanced CT imaging of the abdomen and pelvis was performed following oral and intravenous administration of contrast material. Findings reveal an enlarged, inflamed appendix with peri-appendiceal fat stranding and a fluid-filled appendiceal lumen, consistent with acute appendicitis. No evidence of perforation or abscess formation noted. The remainder of the intra-abdominal structures appears unremarkable.

CONCLUSIONS:

CT findings consistent with acute appendicitis.
No evidence of appendiceal perforation or abscess formation.
