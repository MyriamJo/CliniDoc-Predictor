Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with intermittent rectal bleeding and suspected internal hemorrhoids.

Operative Details:

Utilizing a lubricated anoscope, a thorough examination of the anal canal was performed. Visualization revealed evidence of internal hemorrhoids with occasional areas of mucosal friability. Biopsies were not deemed necessary given the characteristic appearance consistent with benign hemorrhoidal disease.

Impression:

Anoscopy findings consistent with internal hemorrhoids and mucosal friability.

Postoperative Care:

Post-procedurally, the patient was provided with comprehensive instructions for care. This included dietary recommendations to maintain regular bowel habits and minimize straining. Additionally, medicated wipes and ointments were prescribed to alleviate discomfort and promote healing. Lifestyle modifications, such as avoiding prolonged sitting and heavy lifting, were advised.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was arranged to assess treatment response and discuss further management options if necessary. Continued monitoring for bleeding and changes in symptoms was emphasized to ensure optimal outcomes and patient satisfaction.
