Procedure: Anoscopy

Reason for Procedure:

The patient presented with persistent rectal discomfort and concern for underlying pathology.

Operative Details:

Anoscopy was conducted using a lubricated anoscope, gently inserted into the anal canal for direct visualization of the rectal mucosa. Examination revealed evidence of inflamed hemorrhoids with small fissures noted along the anal verge. Gentle pressure was applied to assess for tenderness and bleeding.

Impression:

Anoscopy confirmed the presence of inflamed hemorrhoids with associated fissures.

Postoperative Care:

Following the procedure, the patient received instructions for postoperative care. This included dietary recommendations to increase fiber intake, ensuring soft stools to alleviate strain on the anal canal. Additionally, sitz baths and topical analgesics were recommended to provide symptomatic relief. Proper anal hygiene practices were emphasized to prevent further irritation.

Follow-up:

A follow-up appointment was scheduled to monitor symptom resolution and discuss potential treatment options for hemorrhoids, including lifestyle modifications and medical interventions if necessary.
