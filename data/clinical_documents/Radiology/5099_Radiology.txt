INDICATIONS:
Assessment of suspected appendicitis in a patient with right lower quadrant pain.
PROCEDURE DONE:
CT Abdomen and Pelvis with Oral and Intravenous Contrast.

CT RESULTS:
Contrast-enhanced CT imaging of the abdomen and pelvis was performed. Findings reveal an enlarged and inflamed appendix with peri-appendiceal fat stranding, consistent with acute appendicitis. No evidence of perforation or abscess formation observed. Additionally, no other significant intra-abdominal pathology noted.

CONCLUSIONS:

Enlarged and inflamed appendix with peri-appendiceal fat stranding, consistent with acute appendicitis.
Absence of perforation or abscess formation.
Surgical consultation recommended for consideration of appendectomy.
