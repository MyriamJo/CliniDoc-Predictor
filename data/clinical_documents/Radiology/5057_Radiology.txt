EXAM:
MRI Knee without Contrast.

REASON FOR EXAM:

Evaluation of knee pain and suspected meniscal injury.

TECHNIQUE:

Sagittal, coronal, and axial T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and proton density images of the knee joint are obtained.

FINDINGS:

Mild joint effusion without evidence of significant synovial proliferation. No evidence of acute ligamentous injury or complete ligament tear. There is a complex tear involving the posterior horn of the medial meniscus. Mild chondral thinning in the medial compartment. No evidence of bone marrow edema or subchondral cysts. Patellar alignment is normal.

IMPRESSION:

Complex tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus. Mild degenerative changes in the medial compartment. Clinical correlation recommended for further management.
