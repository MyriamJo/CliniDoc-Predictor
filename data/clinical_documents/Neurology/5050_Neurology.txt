TITLE OF OPERATION:

Electromyography-guided Botulinum Toxin Injection for Treatment of Brachial Plexopathy

INDICATION FOR PROCEDURE:

A 45-year-old male presented with progressive weakness and pain in the left upper limb, suggestive of brachial plexopathy. Initial neurological examination revealed muscle atrophy and reduced strength in the affected limb. Electromyography (EMG) confirmed denervation in the muscles innervated by the brachial plexus, indicating nerve dysfunction.

PREPROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS:

Left brachial plexopathy.

POSTPROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS:

Left brachial plexopathy.

PROCEDURE:

The patient was positioned comfortably in a supine position on the examination table. Following standard aseptic technique, surface electrodes were placed over target muscles, including the deltoid, biceps, triceps, and forearm muscles innervated by the affected nerves. Needle electrodes were inserted into the muscles for precise EMG recording.

Under real-time EMG guidance, Botulinum toxin was injected into the affected muscles to selectively weaken overactive muscles contributing to pain and spasticity. Multiple injection sites were determined based on EMG findings, ensuring optimal distribution of the medication. The procedure was performed smoothly with minimal discomfort to the patient.

POSTPROCEDURE MANAGEMENT:

The patient was advised on post-injection care, including rest and avoidance of strenuous activities for the next 24 hours. Follow-up appointments were scheduled to assess treatment efficacy and plan for further interventions as needed.
