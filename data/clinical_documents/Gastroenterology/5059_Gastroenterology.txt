Medical Record:

Procedure: Pediatric Colonoscopy with Mucosal Biopsy

Reason for Procedure:

Chronic diarrhea and unexplained weight loss in a pediatric patient.

Operative Details:

After obtaining parental consent and ensuring appropriate sedation, the pediatric patient was positioned comfortably for the procedure. The colonoscope was gently inserted through the rectum. Mucosal examination revealed diffuse inflammation, loss of vascular pattern, and areas of friability, particularly in the terminal ileum and ascending colon.

Multiple mucosal biopsies were obtained using cold forceps from affected areas for histopathological examination to investigate the underlying cause of inflammation, such as inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), infectious colitis, or other gastrointestinal disorders.

Impression:

Colonoscopy findings suggestive of mucosal inflammation in the pediatric patient, with evidence of diffuse inflammation and friability in the terminal ileum and ascending colon. Biopsy samples obtained for further evaluation and diagnosis.

Post-procedure Care:

The pediatric patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of post-procedure complications. Instructions were provided to parents regarding post-biopsy care, including diet modifications and monitoring for signs of bleeding or perforation. Follow-up appointment scheduled for discussion of biopsy results and formulation of appropriate treatment plan tailored to the child's condition.
