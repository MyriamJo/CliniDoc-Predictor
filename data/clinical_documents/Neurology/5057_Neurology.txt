Title of Procedure: 
Electromyography-Guided Neuromuscular Reeducation for Peroneal Nerve Palsy

Indication for Procedure:

A 30-year-old female presented with foot drop and weakness in her right lower limb following trauma. Clinical examination revealed reduced dorsiflexion and eversion strength. Electromyography (EMG) was recommended to assess the severity of nerve injury and guide targeted rehabilitation.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Right peroneal nerve palsy.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Right peroneal nerve palsy.

Procedure:

The patient was positioned comfortably in a supine position. Following skin preparation, needle electrodes were inserted into the affected muscles, including the tibialis anterior and peroneus longus, to assess their electrical activity and degree of denervation.

Under real-time EMG guidance, neuromuscular reeducation techniques were employed to facilitate muscle activation and restore proper movement patterns. Visual and auditory feedback were provided to enhance motor learning and improve muscle recruitment.

Impression:

EMG findings support the diagnosis of right peroneal nerve palsy. Electromyography-guided neuromuscular reeducation performed for targeted rehabilitation.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was instructed to continue with prescribed exercises and functional activities at home. Regular physical therapy sessions were recommended to optimize muscle strength and motor function. Follow-up appointments were scheduled to monitor progress and adjust the rehabilitation plan as needed.
