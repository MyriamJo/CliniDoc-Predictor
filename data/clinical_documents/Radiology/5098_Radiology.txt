INDICATIONS:
Evaluation of persistent facial pain and numbness.
PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Facial Nerve with Cranial Nerve Protocol.

MRI RESULTS:
Multiplanar T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and contrast-enhanced sequences of the facial nerve and cranial nerves were obtained. Findings reveal a small enhancing lesion along the course of the left trigeminal nerve at the level of the pontine cistern, suggestive of a possible neurovascular conflict. No evidence of mass effect on adjacent structures noted.

CONCLUSIONS:

Small enhancing lesion along the left trigeminal nerve suggestive of neurovascular conflict.
No evidence of mass effect on adjacent structures.
Clinical correlation recommended for further evaluation of facial pain and numbness.
