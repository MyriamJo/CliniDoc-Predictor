EXAM:
CT Angiography Chest.
REASON FOR EXAM:
Evaluation of suspected pulmonary embolism.

TECHNIQUE:
CT angiography of the chest performed following intravenous administration of contrast material.

FINDINGS:
No evidence of filling defects or pulmonary emboli identified within the pulmonary arteries. Normal enhancement of the pulmonary vasculature. Lung parenchyma appears clear bilaterally. No evidence of pneumothorax or pleural effusion.

IMPRESSION:
Negative CT angiography for pulmonary embolism. No acute cardiopulmonary abnormalities detected. Clinical correlation recommended for further evaluation of symptoms.
