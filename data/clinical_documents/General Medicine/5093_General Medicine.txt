HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:
A 38-year-old female presents with complaints of chronic fatigue, generalized weakness, and muscle aches persisting for the past six months. She reports feeling tired and exhausted despite adequate sleep and rest. She denies any fever, chills, or recent illnesses. Symptoms have progressively worsened over time, impacting her daily activities and work performance.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY:
History of hypothyroidism, managed with levothyroxine supplementation.

PAST SURGICAL HISTORY:
Nonsignificant.

FAMILY HISTORY:
Negative for any significant medical conditions.

SOCIAL HISTORY:
Works as an office manager, non-smoker, occasional alcohol use.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION:
Vital Signs: Within normal limits.
General: Fatigued appearance, no acute distress.
Musculoskeletal: Decreased muscle strength on manual muscle testing, no focal neurological deficits.

ASSESSMENT:

Chronic Fatigue Syndrome: Suspected diagnosis based on the patient's history of persistent fatigue, generalized weakness, and absence of identifiable medical causes.
PLAN:

Diagnostic Workup: 
Order comprehensive laboratory tests including complete blood count, thyroid function tests, and metabolic panel to rule out underlying medical conditions contributing to fatigue.

Symptomatic Management: 
Recommend lifestyle modifications, stress reduction techniques, and regular exercise to improve energy levels and overall well-being.

Psychological Support: 
Consider referral to a psychologist or counselor for cognitive-behavioral therapy to address coping strategies and psychological aspects of chronic fatigue.

Follow-up: 
Schedule a follow-up appointment in one month to review test results and assess response to treatment interventions.
