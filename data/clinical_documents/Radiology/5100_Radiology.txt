Indications:
Evaluation of suspected hepatic lesions, including focal nodular hyperplasia or hepatic adenoma.

Procedure Done:
MRI Liver with Gadolinium Contrast Injection.

MRI Results:

MRI imaging of the liver was performed following the intravenous administration of gadolinium-based contrast material. Multiplanar T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and diffusion-weighted sequences were obtained. The imaging reveals a 3 cm hypervascular lesion in segment IV of the liver with characteristic arterial phase enhancement and central scar seen in the delayed phase, consistent with focal nodular hyperplasia (FNH). No additional lesions were identified. The liver parenchyma appears otherwise normal, with no evidence of cirrhosis or fatty infiltration. The biliary ducts and vasculature are unremarkable.

Conclusions:

MRI findings consistent with a 3 cm focal nodular hyperplasia (FNH) in segment IV of the liver. No additional hepatic lesions or abnormalities are noted. The liver parenchyma, biliary ducts, and vasculature appear normal.
