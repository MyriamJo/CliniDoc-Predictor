INDICATIONS:
Evaluation of chronic right upper quadrant abdominal pain and elevated liver enzymes.

PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Abdomen with MRCP (Magnetic Resonance Cholangiopancreatography).

MRI RESULTS:
Multiplanar T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and MRCP sequences of the abdomen were obtained. Findings reveal hepatomegaly with heterogeneous liver parenchyma, consistent with fatty liver disease. No evidence of focal hepatic lesions or biliary ductal dilatation noted. Additionally, MRCP demonstrates patent intrahepatic and extrahepatic bile ducts without evidence of biliary obstruction or choledocholithiasis.

CONCLUSIONS:

Hepatomegaly with heterogeneous liver parenchyma, suggestive of fatty liver disease.
Normal intrahepatic and extrahepatic bile ducts without evidence of biliary obstruction or choledocholithiasis.
Further evaluation may be warranted to assess the underlying etiology of elevated liver enzymes.
