INDICATIONS:
Assessment of suspected sacroiliitis in a patient with inflammatory back pain.
PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Pelvis with Sacroiliac Joint Protocol.

MRI RESULTS:
Multiplanar T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and fat-suppressed sequences of the pelvis were obtained. Findings reveal asymmetric sacroiliac joint involvement with active inflammatory changes on the left side, including bone marrow edema and joint effusion. No evidence of erosions or subchondral sclerosis observed. Additionally, mild synovitis noted in the adjacent hip joints.

CONCLUSIONS:

Asymmetric sacroiliac joint involvement with active inflammatory changes on the left side.
Absence of erosions or subchondral sclerosis.
Mild synovitis in the adjacent hip joints.
