INDICATIONS:

Acute onset dyspnea, history of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and tobacco abuse.

PROCEDURE DONE:

Ventilation-Perfusion (V/Q) Lung Scan.

VENTILATION SCAN RESULTS:

Ventilation scan was performed using inhaled Xenon-133 gas. Uniform distribution of ventilation throughout both lungs was noted. No focal ventilation defects observed.

PERFUSION SCAN RESULTS:

Perfusion scan was performed using intravenous injection of 4 mCi of Tc-99m macroaggregated albumin. No evidence of segmental or lobar perfusion defects noted. Normal perfusion pattern observed in both lungs.

CONCLUSIONS:

Normal ventilation and perfusion patterns observed, ruling out significant pulmonary embolism.

No evidence of mismatched ventilation-perfusion defects suggestive of acute pulmonary embolism.s
