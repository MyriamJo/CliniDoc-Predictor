INDICATIONS:
Chronic right shoulder pain, limited range of motion, and history of trauma.
PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Shoulder with Arthrogram.

MRI RESULTS:
Contrast-enhanced MRI of the right shoulder was performed using intra-articular injection of gadolinium-based contrast material. Imaging reveals a full-thickness tear of the supraspinatus tendon with retraction, contributing to the patient's symptoms. Additionally, moderate subacromial-subdeltoid bursitis is noted, indicative of ongoing inflammation. Importantly, there is no evidence of labral tear or significant glenohumeral joint abnormalities.

CONCLUSIONS:

Full-thickness tear of the supraspinatus tendon with retraction, likely contributing to shoulder pain and dysfunction.
Moderate subacromial-subdeltoid bursitis, suggestive of ongoing inflammatory process.
Absence of significant glenohumeral joint pathology requiring surgical intervention.
