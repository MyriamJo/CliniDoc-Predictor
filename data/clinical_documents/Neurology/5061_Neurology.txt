Title of Procedure: 
Electromyography-Guided Intramuscular Stimulation for Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome

Indication for Procedure:

A 50-year-old male presented with chronic pelvic pain syndrome characterized by pelvic floor muscle dysfunction and associated urinary symptoms. Clinical examination revealed pelvic floor muscle tenderness and hypertonicity. Electromyography (EMG) was recommended to evaluate muscle function and guide targeted treatment.

Preprocedure Diagnosis:

Chronic pelvic pain syndrome with pelvic floor muscle dysfunction.

Postprocedure Diagnosis:

Chronic pelvic pain syndrome with pelvic floor muscle dysfunction.

Procedure:

The patient was positioned comfortably in a supine position. Following skin preparation, needle electrodes were inserted into the pelvic floor muscles to assess their electrical activity and identify dysfunctional areas.

Under real-time EMG guidance, intramuscular stimulation (IMS) was performed using a fine acupuncture needle inserted into the hypertonic muscle fibers. IMS involves the application of a low-frequency electrical current to stimulate muscle relaxation and reduce pain perception.

Procedure Details:

During the procedure, the patient was instructed to provide feedback on sensation and discomfort to ensure accurate needle placement and optimal treatment effect. Muscle responses were monitored to assess the efficacy of the stimulation and guide adjustment of the treatment parameters.

Impression:

EMG findings support the diagnosis of chronic pelvic pain syndrome with pelvic floor muscle dysfunction. Electromyography-guided intramuscular stimulation performed for targeted pain relief and muscle relaxation.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was educated on post-procedural care, including pelvic floor relaxation exercises and bladder retraining techniques. Regular follow-up appointments were scheduled to monitor symptom improvement and adjust the treatment plan as needed. Rehabilitation therapy, including pelvic floor physical therapy, may be recommended to optimize long-term outcomes.
