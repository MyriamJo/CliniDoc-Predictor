INDICATIONS:
Evaluation of suspected acromegaly in a patient with characteristic clinical features.
PROCEDURE DONE:
MRI Brain with Pituitary Protocol.

MRI RESULTS:
Multiplanar T1-weighted sequences of the brain were obtained before and after intravenous contrast administration. Findings reveal a well-defined contrast-enhancing lesion within the sella turcica measuring approximately 1.2 cm, consistent with a pituitary macroadenoma. No evidence of mass effect on the optic chiasm or surrounding structures observed.

CONCLUSIONS:

Well-defined contrast-enhancing lesion within the sella turcica consistent with a pituitary macroadenoma.
Absence of mass effect on the optic chiasm or surrounding structures.
Clinical correlation recommended for further evaluation of suspected acromegaly.
