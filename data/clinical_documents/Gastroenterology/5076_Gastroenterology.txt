Procedure: Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)

Reason for Procedure:

The patient was diagnosed with chronic acalculous cholecystitis after a prolonged course of right upper quadrant pain and tenderness.

Operative Details:

Elective laparoscopic cholecystectomy was performed. The surgeon meticulously dissected and removed the gallbladder, with attention to preserving surrounding structures.

Impression:

Successful laparoscopic cholecystectomy carried out for chronic acalculous cholecystitis, aiming to relieve symptoms and prevent complications.

Postoperative Care:

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of postoperative complications such as bleeding, infection, or bile leakage. Pain management and intravenous fluids were administered as needed to ensure comfort and hydration.

Instructions were provided for postoperative care, including gradual advancement of diet and avoidance of heavy lifting or strenuous activity during the initial recovery period.

Follow-up:

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment with the surgical team to assess postoperative progress, review pathology results, and address any concerns or questions regarding the procedure. Long-term follow-up may involve monitoring for potential complications such as recurrent symptoms or bile duct injury.
