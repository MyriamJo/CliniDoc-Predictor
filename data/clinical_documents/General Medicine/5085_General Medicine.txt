Chief Complaint:
The patient presents with a persistent rash on the arms and legs.

History of Present Illness:
A 45-year-old male reports developing a pruritic rash on his arms and legs approximately two months ago. The rash initially presented as small red papules, which have since progressed to raised, scaly plaques. He describes the lesions as intensely itchy, especially at night, leading to disrupted sleep. He denies any history of recent insect bites or exposure to irritants. Over-the-counter topical creams have provided temporary relief but failed to resolve the rash.

Past Medical History:
No significant past medical history reported. No history of allergic reactions or chronic skin conditions.

Family History:
Negative for dermatological disorders.

Review of Systems:
Negative for fever, joint pain, or respiratory symptoms. No gastrointestinal or genitourinary symptoms reported.

Physical Examination:
Vital Signs: Within normal limits.
Dermatological: Inspection reveals multiple erythematous papules and plaques with overlying scale distributed symmetrically on the extensor surfaces of the arms and legs. No signs of infection or drainage noted.

Assessment:

Chronic Dermatitis: Suspected diagnosis based on the patient's history of persistent pruritic rash with characteristic morphology and distribution.
Differential Diagnosis: Consideration given to conditions such as eczema, psoriasis, or allergic contact dermatitis.
Plan:

Skin Biopsy: 
Recommend performing a skin biopsy to confirm the diagnosis and rule out underlying pathology.

Topical Treatment: 
Prescribe a potent topical corticosteroid cream for anti-inflammatory and antipruritic effects.

Emollients: 
Advise regular use of emollient creams or ointments to moisturize and soothe the affected skin.

Sun Protection: 
Educate the patient on the importance of sun protection measures, including wearing protective clothing and applying broad-spectrum sunscreen.

Follow-up: 
Schedule a follow-up appointment in six weeks to assess response to treatment and perform a review of the biopsy results.
