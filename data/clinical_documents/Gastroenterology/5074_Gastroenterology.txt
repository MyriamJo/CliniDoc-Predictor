Procedure Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)

Reason for Procedure

The patient had a history of recurrent gallstone pancreatitis and ongoing right upper quadrant pain.

Operative Details

Elective laparoscopic cholecystectomy was performed. The surgeon carefully dissected and removed the gallbladder, aiming to prevent further episodes of pancreatitis.

Impression

Laparoscopic cholecystectomy completed successfully for recurrent gallstone pancreatitis, aiming to prevent future complications.

Postoperative Care

The patient was monitored closely in the recovery area for any signs of postoperative complications such as bleeding, infection, or bile leakage. Pain management and intravenous fluids were administered as needed to ensure comfort and hydration.

Instructions were provided for postoperative care, including gradual advancement of diet and avoidance of heavy lifting or strenuous activity during the initial recovery period.

Follow-up

The patient was scheduled for a follow-up appointment with the surgical team to assess postoperative progress, review pathology results, and address any concerns or questions regarding the procedure. Long-term follow-up may involve monitoring for potential complications such as bile duct injury or recurrent pancreatitis.
